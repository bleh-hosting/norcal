ALIGNMENT 101

7 Signs Your Semi Truck Needs an Alignment

If you’re seeing any of these symptoms on your tractor or trailer, it’s time to schedule mobile service before tire wear accelerates.
  • Vehicle pulls to one side on straight roads
  • Uneven or feathered tire wear (inside or outside edges)
  • Steering wheel vibration or shimmy at highway speeds
  • Excessive tire cupping or “scalloped” tread
  • Recent tire replacement or suspension work
  • Trailer “dog tracking” or not following straight behind tractor
  • Lower than expected fuel economy
Early detection prevents thousands in premature tire and fuel costs.

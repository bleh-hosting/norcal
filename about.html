The NorCal Truck Alignment Story

Miguel started NorCal Truck Alignment after years working in heavy-duty repair and fleet maintenance. He saw the same problems over and over: trucks and trailers being sent to distant shops for alignments, losing days of revenue, and then coming back with the same tire wear issues because the work wasn’t precise enough or the vehicle went right back into the same operating conditions.

He built the business around one core idea: bring the best portable alignment technology directly to the customer — fleets, owner-operators, construction yards, and even roadside when urgent — with zero compromise on accuracy.

Today the business remains owner-operated. Miguel personally performs every alignment. This guarantees consistency, accountability, and the kind of attention to detail that only comes when the person doing the work also owns the reputation.
